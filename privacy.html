Money Diary – Expense Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Money Diary – Expense Manager is a personal finance diary for recording income, expenses and savings. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, the app stores your email address and the name you choose. Financial entries you add may include amount, category, payment mode, date and optional notes.

2. How we use information

Your information is used to authenticate your account, sync your diary across devices, calculate balances and generate reports. We do not use your financial entries for advertising.

3. Cloud storage

Account and diary data are stored using Supabase cloud infrastructure. Row-level security is used so authenticated users can access only their own diary records.

4. Local storage and backups

The app may keep limited legacy data in your device's local storage. The Export feature creates a JSON backup on your device when you request it. You control where that exported file is stored or shared.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal or financial information. If you choose to share a report through another app such as WhatsApp, that sharing is controlled by you and the receiving service's privacy policy applies.

6. Security

The app uses Supabase Authentication and row-level security for cloud data. No internet application can guarantee absolute security, so keep your password private and use a strong password.

7. Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account from Settings inside the app. Account deletion removes the authentication account and associated diary data. This action cannot be undone.

8. Children's privacy

Money Diary – Expense Manager is intended for general personal finance use and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact
